# Cinematography Bible

## Cinematography is a system
Cinematography = lens + position + framing + light + movement + focus + exposure + time.

## Shot sizes
EWS, VWS, WS, FS, MFS, Cowboy, MS, MCU, CU, BCU, ECU, Insert, Macro.

Define body crop, not only label.

## Composition
- center
- thirds
- symmetry
- asymmetry
- negative space
- frame within frame
- foreground layering
- leading lines
- deep staging
- silhouette
- reflection

## Lens language
14–18mm: extreme spatial expansion  
21–24mm: dynamic wide  
28mm: immersive wide  
35mm: environmental narrative  
40–50mm: natural  
65mm: cinematic portrait  
85mm: intimate portrait  
100–135mm: compressed  
200mm+: distant / observational

## Depth
- deep focus
- moderate depth
- shallow
- very shallow
- macro-thin

## Focus
- fixed
- follow
- rack
- pull
- zone
- reveal
- deliberate defocus

## Exposure and motion rendering
Consider:
- aperture
- shutter behavior
- ISO / gain
- highlight protection
- skin exposure
- motion blur character

## Lens continuity
Use a coherent lens family across a project unless deviation is motivated.
