# Color Bible

## Color is narrative
Define:
- primary palette
- accent palette
- saturation range
- contrast
- black level
- highlight character
- skin treatment
- warm/cool relationship

## Color script
Map emotional progression across scenes.

Example:
neutral warm → cooler separation → desaturated crisis → restrained warmth in resolution

## Continuity
Wardrobe, props, environment, and lighting should not accidentally shift palette between adjacent shots.

## Avoid
- arbitrary teal-orange
- random LUT changes
- oversaturated skin
- unexplained white-balance shifts
