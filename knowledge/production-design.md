# Production Design Bible

## World categories
- architecture
- interior layout
- furniture
- props
- materials
- surfaces
- technology
- signage
- vehicles
- environment
- weather
- wear / aging

## Canonical assets
Give recurring objects stable IDs:
CHAR_01
WARDROBE_02
PROP_PHONE_01
LOC_STATION_01

## Location geography
Record:
- doors
- windows
- furniture
- stairs
- exits
- major landmarks
- actor paths
- camera-accessible zones

## Material behavior
Define how surfaces respond to:
- light
- water
- dust
- age
- reflections

## Rule
Do not let generative convenience rewrite continuity-critical geometry.
