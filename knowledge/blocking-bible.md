# Blocking Bible

## Blocking defines
- who is where
- who faces whom
- who moves
- when they move
- why they move
- what object or person they interact with

## Required state
For each character:
- start coordinate or zone
- body orientation
- gaze target
- supporting leg
- movement path
- end coordinate
- interaction points

## Relationship blocking
Use distance and orientation to express:
- intimacy
- conflict
- avoidance
- hierarchy
- isolation

## Screen direction
Track left-to-right and right-to-left travel.
Do not reverse screen direction accidentally.

## Entrance and exit
Define exact frame side or spatial path.

## Contact choreography
When two characters or a prop interact:
- define approach
- define contact
- define weight / pressure
- define release
- avoid simultaneous complex actions where unnecessary
