# Editing Bible

## Edit decisions
- why cut
- when cut
- what changes across cut
- what visual or emotional continuity is preserved

## Common tools
- cut on action
- reaction cut
- J-cut
- L-cut
- match cut
- graphic match
- movement match
- eyeline match
- hard cut
- montage
- jump cut
- hold

## Rhythm
Track:
- average shot length
- density of cuts
- escalation
- release
- silence
- music beat relationship

## Coverage
When dialogue or performance matters, consider:
- master
- medium
- close
- reaction
- insert
- cutaway

## Rule
A visually impressive shot that cannot cut cleanly may be a poor production choice.
