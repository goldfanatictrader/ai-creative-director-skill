# Motion Bible

## Motion layers
1. camera motion
2. subject motion
3. secondary body motion
4. environmental motion
5. optical motion
6. temporal motion

## Motion variables
- direction
- path
- speed
- acceleration
- deceleration
- inertia
- duration
- amplitude
- repetition

## Paths
- linear
- diagonal
- arc
- circular
- vertical
- spiral
- irregular

## Speed vocabulary
imperceptible → very slow → slow → moderate → brisk → fast → rapid → whip-fast

## Secondary motion
Hair, loose clothing, straps, props, and soft tissue should lag primary motion slightly.

## Environmental motion
Wind, rain, haze, smoke, traffic, crowds, leaves, water.

## Relative motion
A subject can approach while the camera:
- remains static
- tracks backward
- pushes forward
- side-tracks
- arcs

Each creates a different visual relationship.

## Motion budget
Reduce simultaneous motion layers when:
- faces must remain consistent
- hands interact
- multiple characters interact
- camera path is complex
