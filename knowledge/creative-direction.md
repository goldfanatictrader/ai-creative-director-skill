# Creative Direction Bible

## Purpose
Translate a brief into a distinct creative system.

## Core decisions
- audience
- message
- desired feeling
- memorable image or idea
- creative territory
- storytelling mode
- realism level
- tone
- pace
- visual density
- degree of stylization
- brand or narrative constraints

## Concept framework
A concept should be expressible in one sentence and visibly affect:
- story
- casting
- production design
- camera
- performance
- lighting
- sound
- editing

If the concept changes none of these, it is probably only a theme label.

## Storytelling modes
- narrative
- observational
- documentary
- performance
- montage
- surreal
- symbolic
- product-driven
- character-driven
- experiential

## Visual grammar
Lock a project-specific grammar:
- preferred shot sizes
- preferred lens family
- movement philosophy
- handheld rules
- color logic
- composition logic
- transition logic
- forbidden visual clichés

## Creative restraint
Do not use every available technique.
A strong project has repeated rules and deliberate exceptions.
