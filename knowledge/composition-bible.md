# Composition Bible

## Primary controls
- subject placement
- scale
- headroom
- lead room
- horizon
- balance
- negative space
- visual weight
- foreground
- midground
- background

## Depth design
Create depth through:
- overlap
- scale difference
- parallax
- atmospheric perspective
- focus separation
- luminance separation
- color separation

## Psychological use
Centering: authority, formality, confrontation  
Negative space: isolation, anticipation  
Compression: pressure, intimacy, surveillance  
Wide depth: freedom, vulnerability, spatial awareness

## Continuity
Composition should cut coherently:
- maintain screen direction
- avoid accidental jump in subject scale
- preserve eyeline
- plan match geometry when needed
