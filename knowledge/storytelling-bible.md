# Storytelling Bible

## Core structure
Every story requires:
- desire
- obstacle
- change

## Sequence model
Hook → Setup → Escalation → Turn → Payoff → Resolution

Not every short video needs traditional acts, but every sequence needs progression.

## Scene definition
A scene must contain:
- objective
- obstacle or tension
- action
- reaction
- state change

## Beat definition
A beat is the smallest meaningful change in:
- intention
- information
- power
- emotion
- action

## Character arc
Track:
- starting belief
- pressure
- contradiction
- realization
- final state

## Visual storytelling
Prefer showing over explanatory dialogue when the information can be communicated through:
- action
- composition
- prop state
- spatial distance
- lighting
- silence
- reaction

## Setup and payoff
Record meaningful setup items and their intended payoff.
Do not create unexplained visual emphasis unless it is atmosphere by design.
