# Sound Bible

## Sound layers
- production dialogue
- ADR / replacement dialogue
- room tone
- ambience
- Foley
- spot effects
- off-screen sound
- music
- silence

## Perspective
Sound changes with:
- distance
- obstruction
- room size
- camera / listener position
- indoor / outdoor acoustics

## Narrative sound
Use sound to:
- reveal off-screen events
- create anticipation
- bridge cuts
- motivate character reaction
- create contrast with image

## Music
Define:
- start point
- stop point
- motif
- rhythm
- dynamics
- sync hits
- when silence is stronger

## Rule
Do not treat sound as an afterthought after picture generation.
