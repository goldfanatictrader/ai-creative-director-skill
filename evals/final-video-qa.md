# Eval — Final Video QA

PASS only when:
- whole-video temporal review was used when supported
- audio-review availability is explicit
- localized findings use timecodes
- inter-shot identity/scale/prop/environment continuity was checked
- motion/pacing were evaluated temporally
- technical claims were measured when tooling was available
- findings distinguish observed / measured / inferred
- visual choppiness was not automatically called FPS drop
- audible distortion was not automatically called clipping
- stereo was not treated as proof of spatialization
- blocker/major findings have a repair route

Verdict: PASS / CONDITIONAL / FAIL.
