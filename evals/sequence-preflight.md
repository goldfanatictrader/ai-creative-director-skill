# Eval — Sequence Preflight

Run before generating a multi-shot sequence.

## Story
- every shot has a purpose and motivation
- emotional beat has setup/change/reaction/hold where needed
- ending/payoff is planned

## Identity and scale
- canonical recurring assets locked
- distinctive markings locked
- proportions and scale anchors locked
- planned angles/actions/lighting are within tested survivability

## Props and locations
- recurring props have canonical IDs/state
- flexible props preserve identity/material
- location geography/anchors locked

## Time/weather/light
- progression is explicit
- wetness/dirt/damage states defined
- no unexplained warm/cold/time-of-day jumps

## Performance/behavior
- primary action defined
- locomotion/contact risks handled
- animal/creature behavior validated where applicable
- environment interaction defined

## Camera/style
- shot/lens/DOF/camera-motion grammar coherent
- deliberate variation distinguished from drift

## Audio
- ambience bed defined
- Foley obligations identified
- visible vocalization sync strategy defined
- transition/outro audio behavior planned

## Continuity bridges
Every adjacent pair has a valid end-state → start-state bridge across identity, scale, props, location, weather/light, emotion, and ambience.

## Verdict
PASS — proceed.
CONDITIONAL — only documented non-blocking assumptions remain.
FAIL — do not begin expensive generation.
