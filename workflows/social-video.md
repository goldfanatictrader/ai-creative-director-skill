# Workflow — Short-Form Social Video

1. define first-2-second hook
2. define target watch behavior
3. define message
4. choose one dominant visual idea
5. structure: hook → escalation → payoff
6. design readable framing for target aspect ratio
7. minimize narrative overhead
8. use high information density without overloading motion
9. plan captions / safe zones if needed
10. optimize shot durations for pacing
