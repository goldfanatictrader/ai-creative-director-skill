# Workflow — Concept Development

1. Parse brief.
2. Identify audience, objective, message, desired emotion.
3. Identify the actual creative problem.
4. Generate 2–4 genuinely different territories if exploration is needed.
5. Select or refine one direction.
6. Define the concept in one sentence.
7. Define visual and sonic consequences of that concept.
8. Define what the concept explicitly avoids.
9. Validate distinctiveness, relevance, executability, coherence.
10. Hand off into story or sequence design.
